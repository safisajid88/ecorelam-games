Summary

We do not ask you to create an account. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number or precise location. Your game progress and settings stay on your device. The only data collected from your device is what our advertising partner, Google AdMob, collects in order to show ads.

Information stored on your device

Game progress, scores, stars, unlocked levels and settings (sound, vibration, theme, language) are saved locally on your device. We do not receive this data. You can remove it by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Our apps show banner, interstitial and optional rewarded ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and process device and usage information such as the Advertising ID, IP address, device model, operating system version, app interactions and approximate location derived from the IP address. Ads may be personalised or non-personalised depending on your region and your choices.

Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom) we ask for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice later where the app offers it, and you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads).

Learn how Google uses data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

What we do not do

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use third-party analytics or tracking tools beyond AdMob. We do not collect contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera data.

Network use

The app connects to the internet to load ads and, if configured, a small list of our other games ("More games from us"). No personal information is sent by us in these requests.

Children

Our apps are designed for a general audience and are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help.

Data retention and your rights

We do not hold personal data about you on our servers. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data and to opt out of personalised advertising. Because we do not hold such data, requests mostly concern AdMob; you can use the controls described above or contact us and we will point you to the right process.

Security

Local data is protected by your device's security. Data sent to ad providers is sent over encrypted connections where available.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The effective date above will change and the new version will be published at the same address.
