Privacy Policy — Pair.POP

Effective date: November 11, 2026

EcoRealm Games ("we", "us", or "our") publishes Pair.POP, a card-matching puzzle game. This policy explains how information is handled when you use Pair.POP, including information processed by third-party advertising services.

1. Game information and accounts

Pair.POP does not require you to create an account or provide a name or email address to play. The game stores settings and progress, such as scores, unlocked levels, and preferences, locally on your device. This information is used to make the game work and retain your preferences. We do not operate a game account server for this information.

2. Advertising and third-party information collection

Pair.POP uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to display advertisements, including banner, interstitial and optional rewarded advertisements. Depending on device settings, region, consent choices, and SDK configuration, Google or its advertising partners may process:

  • Device or app identifiers, which may include advertising identifiers where permitted;
  • IP address and approximate location derived from IP address;
  • Advertising impressions, clicks, video views and other interactions;
  • App interaction, performance information, crash reports and diagnostic data.

This information may be used to deliver, measure, protect and improve advertisements and related services. Personalized advertising or tracking, where applicable, depends on relevant consent, platform rules and device permissions. We do not receive a list of your personal identifiers from AdMob merely because you play the game.

For details on Google's handling of information, see Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

3. Your privacy choices

Where required, the app or advertising SDK may ask for advertising consent and provide privacy options. You can also control certain advertising permissions through your iOS device settings, including Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, if available. Changing these choices may affect the kinds of advertisements you see, but not the ability to play the core game.

If you would like assistance with a privacy question or request, email safisajid1225@gmail.com. For Google-controlled advertising information, consult Google's privacy tools and policies linked above.

4. Purchases and payments

Pair.POP is offered as a free game with advertisements. The game does not ask you to enter payment card information to play. If purchases are introduced in a future version, we will update this policy as appropriate.

5. Children

Pair.POP is a general-audience puzzle game. We do not intentionally request names, contact details or other identifying information from children. Advertising services may nevertheless process device and usage information as described above. If you believe a child has sent personal information to our support address, contact us and we will review the request.

6. Data retention and security

Locally stored game settings and progress generally remain on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall it. Information held by Google and its partners is subject to their respective policies and retention practices. We take reasonable steps to protect support communications, but no transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when features, SDKs or legal requirements change. The latest version will be posted at this URL with an updated effective date.
